Lesson 3 — Can you come home mirrored?

Read the lesson.

Three 5 × 5 square gluings side by side: A, an adjacent-edge sphere; B, an adjacent-edge projective plane; C, the opposite-edge projective plane. The lesson traces one loop on two boards to see a frame come home mirrored, moves a two-cell piece through the same commands on all three boards to see bonds break and rejoin, and counts blocked moves and lost bonds on an 8 × 8 board to give “worse” a denominator. Follow Chapter 2’s introduction to transported frames first; allow 15–20 minutes.

Files

  • chapter_03_seam_comparisons.md — the lesson, its worked comparisons, the benchmark table and teaching notes.
  • interactive/index.html — the interactive comparison: a shared step control for boards A and B (part 1), the domino on all three boards (part 2), and the 8 × 8 benchmark (part 3, #benchmark). The page loads the scripts and stylesheet beside it:
    • seam_comparison.js — the two-board loop and frame transport.
    • board_rules.js — the three gluings and the board-axis move rule.
    • controls_and_bonds.js — the domino comparison and its bond display.
    • domino_traces.js — the native traces the domino clips are checked against.
    • benchmark.js — the 8 × 8 enumeration of blocked moves and lost bonds.
    • styles.css.
  • interactive/smoke_browser.py — browser smoke test of the interactive page (Playwright, Chromium).
  • domino-comparisons/ — five animated GIFs of the two-cell piece on the three boards, the generator domino.py, the native trace native_traces.txt and the C++ program verify_native.cpp that regenerates it from Tet4D’s native core. See its README.

Run from the repository root

Open learning/tutorials/seam-comparisons/interactive/index.html in a browser; keep the adjacent JavaScript and CSS files together. The smoke test and the clip generator:

python3 learning/tutorials/seam-comparisons/interactive/smoke_browser.py        # needs Playwright + Chromium
cd learning/tutorials/seam-comparisons/domino-comparisons
python3 domino.py --out rendered                                                 # needs Pillow, DejaVu Sans

domino.py checks every clip’s end pose against native_traces.txt. To regenerate the trace from Tet4D’s native core, see the domino README.

Scope

The lesson uses its own board-axis rule (up increases y) and documents how to convert to Explorer’s engine coordinates. The counts are reference results for the stated presets, not global rankings of topologies, and the lesson does not establish authority over the native or Godot implementations; see the Explorer requirements for the engine’s movement and occupancy rules.

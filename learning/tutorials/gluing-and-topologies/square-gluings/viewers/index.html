Square gluings, interactive

The tutorial's six gluings, the classic glass Klein bottle and Boy's surface as in the Boy's surface viewer. Each viewer glues one square into its closed surface. Corners A–D run clockwise from the upper left; the orange edges a close first, then the cyan edges b. Drag the Glue slider or press ▶, set the speed, step frame by frame, rotate at any moment, and switch cone points on or off where the corners meet at less than 360°.

Earth on Tet4D's S² board

Drag the square to rotate Earth beneath the fixed gluing. Land moves across the identified edges without breaking continuity.

Controls

Pointer or touch drag rotates the Earth as a sphere. The square and its seam pairings remain unchanged.

What stays fixed?

  • The square's topological identifications.
  • The two chart triangles, separated by the dotted diagonal.
  • The same Earth point on each pair of identified edge locations.

What changes?

The orientation of Earth relative to the board. The equator and geographical poles rotate with the globe; they are not tied to the triangle boundary after dragging.

Surface versus drawing

The displayed square is not a Euclidean world map. It is a continuous but non-isometric coordinate representation of a sphere. Distortion and visual seam cuts are expected.

Illustrative two-triangle sphere projection; coastlines from Basemap's bundled GSHHS low-resolution data. Land/ocean colouring is stylized. Fully offline, no server or external scripts.
